Questions

How often should I check loft and lie?

Once a year for most golfers, or after a season of range mats or a few hard hits off hardpan. Forged irons move more than cast ones.

Can cast irons be bent?

Usually a degree or two. We check the head before bending and tell you first if a club is at risk.

What does lie angle change?

A lie that is too upright tends to start the ball left (for right-handed golfers) and too flat starts it right. Getting it right helps shots start on line.

Do you adjust wedges and putters?

Wedges, yes, as part of the set. Ask about putters on your request.
