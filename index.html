About the shop

Local, hands-on, fairly priced

Rocket City ClubWorks is a home club shop in Huntsville run by Jackson Stewart, a local golfer who got tired of waiting a week for a simple regrip.

We keep things straightforward: retail prices on parts, clear labor prices, and clubs back in your bag fast. If you're unsure what grip or shaft fits your game, ask. Advice is free.

Common questions
How fast will my clubs be ready?

Next day with your own grips or grips we keep in stock. Special-order grips are ordered every Monday and Thursday and take 3–5 days. Need it sooner? A rush order ships the same day for $10 and takes 2–3 days.

Can I bring my own grips?

Yes. Install is $5 per club or $40 for a full bag with your own grips. Buy grips from us and it's $3 per club or $35 for a full bag.

Do my irons need a loft and lie check?

Lofts and lies drift with play, especially on forged irons, and a club that's a couple degrees off can push shots offline. A check takes about 20 minutes, and we'll show you the numbers before bending anything.

What grip size should I get?

Bring your current club and we'll check your hand size and how you hold it. Build-up wraps to fine-tune size are included at no charge.

How does trade-in credit work?

Bring in shafts, adapters or heads you're not using. We check their condition and quote a credit you can put toward any service. Parts we take in are cleaned up and offered to other golfers as lower-cost repair options.

Where do I drop off clubs?

We meet locally around Huntsville and Madison, or you can drop off at the shop by appointment. We'll confirm the details when you book.
