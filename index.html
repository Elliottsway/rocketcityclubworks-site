About the shop

Local, hands-on, fairly priced

Rocket City ClubWorks is a home club shop in Huntsville run by Jackson Stewart, a local golfer who got tired of waiting a week for a simple regrip.

Clear labor prices, parts at regular retail, and clubs back in your bag fast. Not sure what grip or shaft fits your game? Ask. Advice is free.

How fast will my clubs be ready?

Next day with your own grips or grips we keep in stock (Tour Velvet and MCC). Special-order grips are ordered every Monday and Thursday and take 3–5 days. A rush order ships the same day for $10 and takes 2–3 days.

Can I bring my own grips?

Yes. Install is $5 per club or $40 for a full bag. Buy grips from us and labor drops to $3 per club or $35 for a full bag.

Where do I drop off my clubs?

Drop-off and pickup are by appointment at our shop in northwest Huntsville, or we can meet up around Huntsville and Madison. We send the address when you book.

How do I pay?

Cash, Venmo, Cash App or Apple Cash at pickup.

Do my irons need a loft and lie check?

Lofts and lies drift with play, especially on forged irons, and a club a couple of degrees off can push shots offline. A check takes about 20 minutes, and we show you the numbers before bending anything.

What grip size should I get?

Bring a current club and we'll check your hand size and how you hold it. Build-up wraps to fine-tune size are free.

How does trade-in credit work?

Bring in shafts, adapters or heads you're not using. We check their condition and quote a credit toward any service.
