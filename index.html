About the shop

Local, hands-on, fairly priced

Rocket City ClubWorks is Jackson Stewart's club shop in northwest Huntsville. Every club is done by hand by the same person who answers your ticket: no counter staff, no back room, no week-long wait.

Clear labor prices, parts at regular retail, and clubs back in your bag fast. Not sure what grip or shaft fits your game? Ask. Advice is free.

How fast will my clubs be ready?

Your own grips, loft and lie, and most repairs are ready the next day. Grips and parts we order arrive in about 2 business days, so those jobs take 3–4 business days (3–5 for reshafts, since the epoxy cures overnight). Need it sooner? Rush overnight parts are $25 and cut it to about 2 business days.

Can I bring my own grips?

Yes, and they're ready the next day. Labor is the same either way: $5 per club or $50 for a full bag of up to 14. If we order grips for you, you pay the grip at regular retail plus a $10 parts fee (free when parts total $100+).

Why is there a parts fee?

We don't keep a warehouse of grips and shafts. Everything is ordered for your job from GolfWorks, and the $10 fee covers the shipping. It's free when your parts total $100 or more, and you only pay it once per order.

Where do I drop off my clubs?

Drop-off and pickup are by appointment at our shop in northwest Huntsville, or we can meet up around Huntsville and Madison. We send the address when you book.

How do I pay?

When Jackson sends your final price, you approve it and pay by card right on your service ticket (secure checkout by Square). Parts are ordered as soon as your payment clears.

Do my irons need a loft and lie check?

Lofts and lies drift with play, especially on forged irons, and a club a couple of degrees off can push shots offline. A check takes about 20 minutes, and we show you the numbers before bending anything.

What grip size should I get?

Bring a current club and we'll check your hand size and how you hold it. Build-up wraps to fine-tune size are free.

How does trade-in credit work?

Bring in shafts, adapters or heads you're not using. We check their condition and quote a credit toward any service.
