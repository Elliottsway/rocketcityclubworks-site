Questions

Can you fix a club with a loose or rattling head?

Usually, yes. We pull the head, clean out the old epoxy, re-epoxy it and add a new ferrule for $20. Most are ready the next day.

My shaft snapped. Can it be repaired?

A broken shaft gets replaced, not glued. We match the shaft you had or suggest an upgrade, and price it on your ticket before any work starts.

Can you shorten or lengthen my clubs?

Yes. Shortening is $10 per club and extending is $15 plus the extension and grip. Junior cut-downs welcome.

Can you find a replacement adapter or part?

Most adapters, sleeves and weights can be ordered. Pick "Find a part" on the request form with your club model and a photo.
