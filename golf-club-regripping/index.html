Questions

How much does it cost to regrip a full set of golf clubs?

Labor is $50 for a full bag of up to 14 clubs, your grips or ours. With 13 Golf Pride Tour Velvet grips ordered for you, that's $93.47 in grips + $50 labor + the $10 parts fee, plus sales tax on the grips. The instant price shows the exact total for your grips.

How often should I regrip my clubs?

About once a year, or every 40 rounds, if you play regularly. Regrip sooner if your grips look shiny, feel hard or slick, or show worn spots where your thumbs sit.

Can I bring my own grips?

Yes. Labor is the same either way ($5 per club, $50 for a full bag) and own-grip jobs are ready the next day.

What grip size should I get?

Most golfers fit a standard or midsize grip. Bring a current club and we check your hand size and how you hold it. Build-up wraps to fine-tune the size are free.

How long until I can play them?

Right away. Grips are installed with solvent that sets in a few hours, so they are ready by pickup.
